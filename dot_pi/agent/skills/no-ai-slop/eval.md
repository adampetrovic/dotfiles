# Edit self-check

Run these checks on the edited draft. If any fails, fix the draft and check again.

1. **Meaning.** The edit adds no claim, number, example, source, or opinion that the original lacked. Every specific fact in the original survives.
2. **Voice.** The distinctive vocabulary, humor, bluntness, uncertainty, and cadence of the original survive. Strong human sentences are untouched. Paragraphs are not all the same length or shape.
3. **Banned words.** None of the banned words remain (delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving).
4. **Empty words and phrases.** Remaining adverbs and stock phrases carry emphasis, uncertainty, or the writer's voice.
5. **Patterns.** No binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, faux-insight setups, colon reveals, trailing -ing analysis, importance puffery, interpretive metadiscourse, weasel attribution, negative listing, dramatic fragmentation, or rhetorical setups remain.
6. **Portability.** No sentence could move unchanged to another person, company, or product.
7. **Ending.** The draft ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action. No aphorism, mic-drop, or recap.
8. **Formatting.** No emoji headings, mid-sentence bold, needless bullets, or headers over two-sentence sections. Em dashes: none in short copy, at most two in long drafts.
9. **Output.** The full edited draft is shown, followed by a short What changed section that also explains any reorganisation.
