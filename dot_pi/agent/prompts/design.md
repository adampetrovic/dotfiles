---
description: Research and draft an evidence-based engineering design
argument-hint: "[problem or proposal]"
---
Explore ${@:-the proposed design}. First clarify the problem, constraints, stakeholders, and success criteria from the available context; ask if a decision-critical requirement is missing. Consult relevant code, docs, and current external or internal sources where useful, linking claims to evidence and flagging uncertainty. Compare viable options and trade-offs (complexity, operations, migration, failure modes, security, and cost). Recommend one approach with a phased implementation, validation and rollback plan, unresolved questions, and a short decision summary. Draft locally only; do not publish or change the system unless asked.
